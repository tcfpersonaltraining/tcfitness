The Truth

Most Physical Decline Doesn't Happen Overnight

It happens so gradually that most people don't notice it — until one day they do.

  • You don't get down on the floor as often
  • You think twice before lifting something heavy
  • Your back feels tight after sitting
  • You avoid activities you used to enjoy
  • You feel a little less steady
  • You need longer to recover after being active

Not because you're weak.

Not because you're lazy.

Because life gets busy — and strength, balance, mobility, and endurance slowly fade if we don't intentionally maintain them.

The encouraging news? Most of these changes can be improved. But first, you need to know where you stand.
