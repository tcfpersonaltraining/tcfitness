Why This Is Different

Fitness Isn't Just About What You Can Lift.

Your physical capacity affects the things you want to keep doing — traveling, carrying groceries, climbing stairs, getting off the floor, walking the dog, working in the yard, hiking, and spending time with the people you care about.

This guide gives you a simple starting point for understanding where you are today and identifying an area worth improving.

Is This For You?

This Is For You If…

  • You want to stay active as you get older.
  • You want to maintain your strength and independence.
  • You've noticed changes in your balance, mobility, endurance, or confidence.
  • You've completed physical therapy but aren't sure what to do next.
  • You've had an injury or period of inactivity and want a starting point.
  • You want to understand your physical capacity instead of simply following random workouts.
  • You want to keep doing things like travel, hike, garden, use stairs, or play with your family.
A quick note: The Physical Capacity Check is an educational resource — not a medical evaluation, diagnosis, physical therapy assessment, or personalized training program. If a test feels unsafe or inappropriate for you, skip it.
