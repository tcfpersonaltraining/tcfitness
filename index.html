Common questions

Before you start.

A few straightforward answers. If your question is personal to your situation, include it in the assessment.

Do I need a gym or special equipment?

No. Your recommendation and plan can be shaped around the space and equipment you actually have. The assessment asks about your setup before anything is prescribed.

Is TC Fitness physical therapy?

No. TC Fitness provides personal training and fitness education, not physical therapy or medical care. Trey’s PTA background informs how he thinks about movement, but coaching does not diagnose, treat or rehabilitate an injury or medical condition.

What if I have pain or a medical condition?

Follow the restrictions and guidance of your physician or physical therapist. The assessment helps identify whether fitness coaching may be appropriate. Sharp pain, numbness, tingling, dizziness, chest discomfort or other concerning symptoms should be addressed with a qualified medical provider.

How will we communicate?

Communication is handled asynchronously through email, text and Trainerize. There is no required sales call, making the process easier to fit around your schedule.

Which option should I choose?

You do not have to decide alone. Complete the free assessment first, and Trey will recommend the simplest level of support that fits your goals and situation.

How quickly will I see results?

Progress depends on your starting point, consistency, recovery and many other factors. TC Fitness does not guarantee results; the focus is sustainable progress you can maintain.
